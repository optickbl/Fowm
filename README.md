# Fowm Plan

The Fowm blueprint as a home-screen app.

- **Today**: the freddo cup fills with foam as you finish steps, your Monday check-in streak, what's next, and a badge for every stage.
- **Plan**: seven stages from kitchen tests to selling the brand. Tap any step for exactly how to do it, when it counts as done, and a shortcut to the screen or chapter it needs. Gates, notes on any step and steps of your own.
- **Numbers**: the weekly check-in with trends, the blind taste-test tally, kitchen tests A–D, and calculators for what you keep per order, the budget, what Fowm could sell for and an investor round.
- **Contacts**: manufacturers, cafés and creators, with status, quotes and sales.
- **Guide**: the full blueprint in twelve chapters, with sources.

Everything is stored on the device only. Save a backup file to iCloud Drive from Settings on the Today screen; Today reminds you when the last backup is over two weeks old. Deletes, restores and starting over can be undone. Works offline after the first visit.
